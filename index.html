The mark

Cast in bronze, made to travel.

Our logo is the Lạc bird, the long-beaked bird that circles the face of the Đông Sơn bronze drums, cast in northern Vietnam more than two thousand years ago.

Those drums were carried, traded and heard far from where they were made. That's the idea behind N4.CREATIVES.: careful, hand-finished craft from Vietnam, delivered to real estate teams around the world.

How it works

From upload to delivery.

Four steps, the same every time. After the first project, most clients only do steps one and four.

  1. 01

    Upload

    Share raw footage through Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer. Add your logo, fonts and agent details.

  2. 02

    Brief

    Pick a package and send references or a past video you like. On the first project we build your style sheet.

  3. 03

    Edit & review

    We cut, grade and mix. You review the first version and send timestamped notes, up to two rounds.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Final files arrive named and organized: 4K master, social cutdowns and unbranded versions where needed.
